# Tasks 21–23

## Task 21 — Shadow / Reality Gap / Stress
Paper-vs-observed execution comparison, slippage/latency reporting, unknown/unreconciled stop semantics, common-mode AI decision testing and capacity admission with headroom.

## Task 22 — Governance / Promotion
Material-change gate, operator authorization matrix and human approval requirements for higher-risk promotion. Risk controls remain deterministic and outside AI authority.

## Task 23 — Advanced Intelligence foundation
Independent safety decision layer, common-mode failure containment and explicit unknown-means-stop primitive. This is a foundation for advanced intelligence; it does not enable autonomous live trading.

## Production boundary
No exchange credentials, broker SDK, autonomous live execution, leverage escalation or AI write access to Risk Gate is introduced. Final production audit remains mandatory before any live-money consideration.
